Inside the room

A live room that is easier to run and follow

Quick

One-tap presets

Start a quick auction, a standard one or the full player pool in one tap. Every individual setting is still there.

Pinned

Phone bidding bar

On a phone, the player, countdown, leading bid and big Bid button stay pinned, with your purse and squad progress beneath.

Clear

Live feedback

Plain messages for a bid placed, being outbid, a player sold or unsold, and reconnecting. They read fine with motion off.

Depth

3D auction stage

A dimensional stage, raised bid keys and layered team cards, each in its own league colour.

How it works

Four steps from room code to final grade

01

Open a room

The host picks a quick, standard or full-player preset, or sets the purse, squad size and players, then shares the room code.

02

Claim a team

Each friend takes a franchise. The host can sit back and spectate instead.

03

Bid live

Players come up one by one. Outbid everyone before the timer runs out. Your bid button stays in reach on a phone.

04

Face the grade

Every squad is ranked at the end, with a grade for the best team.
