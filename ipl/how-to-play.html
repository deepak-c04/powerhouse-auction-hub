GETTING STARTED

HOW THE GAME FLOW WORKS

1

Create or join a room

The host opens a room and shares its code. Friends join with the code or browse public rooms, and spectators can watch without bidding.

2

Choose the auction mode

Mega Auction is the big squad-building challenge. Mini Auction is shorter and focuses on the strength of your final XI.

3

Select your franchise

Every bidder locks in a team before the host starts the room. Spectators do not count as bidders.

4

Bid player by player

Each player enters at a base price. Teams bid live while watching their purse, overseas balance and squad shape. A squad can hold at most 8 overseas players, and the auto-picked Playing XI uses at most 4.

5

Build the best XI

When the auction ends, every Playing XI is scored and the teams are ranked on the leaderboard.

AUCTION RULES

CORE RULES EVERY PLAYER SHOULD KNOW

The rules that shape every room before the results are worked out.

  • Each franchise starts with a fixed purse of ₹120 Cr (the room setup also offers ₹150 and ₹200 Cr).
  • A player goes to the highest bidder when the timer runs out.
  • The host can start with one active bidder, but a warning shows until more people join.
  • Spectators watch only. They do not bid and do not count toward the start requirement.
  • Player ratings stay hidden during the live auction, so bidding is a real judgement call.
  • Ratings are revealed afterwards in the Results and Leaderboard views.
MODES

MEGA VS MINI AUCTION

Mega Auction

A full squad-building experience across a large player pool. Your team is judged on its final XI.

Mini Auction

Quicker rooms and tighter competition. Mini mode rewards the strongest balanced XI and checks role balance more strictly.

SCORING & RESULTS

HOW POINTS ARE CALCULATED

You bid without seeing ratings. After the auction, every team is scored from hidden player ratings and its final XI.

Player rating system

Every player has a hidden rating out of 99, used for squad analysis, results and the leaderboard.

Total score

A team total is the sum of the ratings of the players in its evaluated Playing XI.

Average score

The total divided by the number of players used, so squad quality and balance are easy to compare.

Mini Auction validation

Mini mode expects a balanced XI. A team can be disqualified if it misses the minimum batters, bowlers or wicketkeeper.

Mega Auction XI selection

If you have not submitted a final XI, the system can pick the best available one from your squad using ratings and balance rules.

AFTER THE AUCTION

WHAT YOU SEE ON THE RESULTS PAGE

Squad Results

Your final team, purse used and left, total and average rating, and a player-by-player breakdown.

Leaderboard

Every franchise side by side, ranked by Playing XI strength, with the scorecard behind each total.

Hidden ratings reveal

Results are the big reveal, where strategy, risk and value picks finally get judged.

STRATEGY

TIPS TO WIN MORE ROOMS

  • Don't spend too early just because a star player comes up first.
  • Check your rivals' purses before entering a bidding war.
  • A balanced XI usually beats an expensive but lopsided squad.
  • With ratings hidden, hunt for undervalued players, not just famous ones.
  • In multiplayer rooms, timing matters almost as much as budget.
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

How do points work?

Each player has a rating out of 99. After the auction, your Playing XI is scored and team totals and averages appear in Results and the Leaderboard.

Are ratings visible while bidding?

No. They are hidden during the live auction and shown afterwards in the results.

How many players do I need to start?

A host can start with one active bidder, though a warning shows while only one is present.

What's the difference between Mega and Mini?

Mega is the broader squad-building mode. Mini is tighter and XI-focused, with stricter balance checks in the final ranking.
