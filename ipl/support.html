SUPPORT

Quick answers, plus a way to write up a problem so you can send it on.

Who can play the multiplayer auction?

Everyone bids as their own team in the same live room. Players must be signed in to Claude and open the game link with Contributor (or Editor) access, which the owner sets in the share menu. People with view-only access can watch but not bid. Share the room code with your friends so they can join.

Is Power House IPL Auction free?

Yes, there is nothing to pay. Solo practice needs no sign-in. A live room with friends needs everyone signed in to Claude, as explained in the first answer.

Where do the players, base prices and stats come from?

Photos are from the official IPL squad pages. Base prices and career stats were copied from the public iplauction.fun player list. Check for updates before relying on them.

Who is it for?

Cricket fans who want to practise or run a fun auction night. It is a fan-made simulator, not affiliated with the BCCI, the IPL or any franchise.

The page is slow to load. What can I do?

The player photos are saved inside the Players page, so it is the heaviest page. Use Wi-Fi if you can and give it a few seconds. Other pages are light.

My saved rooms or events vanished.

They are stored in this browser. Clearing site data, private browsing, or switching device removes them.

Report a problem

Copies your message with page and browser details so you can paste it to whoever runs the site.
