How it works

I

Open a room

The host sets the purse, squad size and timer, then shares the room code.

II

Claim a team

Each friend takes a franchise. The host can spectate instead.

III

Bid live

Players come up one by one. Outbid everyone before the timer ends.

IV

Face the grade

Every squad gets rated and ranked, with a grade for the best team.

Rules that keep it fair

Cat A, B and C

Base prices of ₹50 L, ₹30 L and ₹10 L. Stars come first, depth last.

Reserve rule

Keep ₹10 L for every empty squad slot so you can always finish your squad.

Overseas caps

At most 6 overseas in a squad and 4 in your Playing XI.

Inside the auction room

Live rooms

One host opens a room and shares a code. Friends join and bid in real time.

Round 2 for unsold

After the auction, players nobody bought come back so you can fill the gaps.

Room chat

Talk to the other bidders from inside the auction room.

Sold and upcoming lists

See who went to which team, who is unsold and who is coming next.

Pass to sell

If one team bids and every other team passes, the player is sold straight away.

Trades

Once the auction ends, teams can swap players with each other.
