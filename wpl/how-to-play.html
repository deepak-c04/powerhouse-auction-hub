HOW TO PLAY

One host opens a room, friends join with the code, each person takes a franchise and bids live for 91 players.

Core rules
  • Purse: ₹15 Cr per franchise.
  • Reserve: keep ₹10 L for every empty squad slot, so you can always finish your squad.
  • Squad: 15, 16 or 18 players, chosen by the host.
  • Overseas: at most 6 in a squad. You cannot bid on a seventh.
  • Base prices: Category A ₹50 L, B ₹30 L, C ₹10 L.
  • Order: Category A players come first, then B, then C.
  • Timer: 8, 12 or 20 seconds. Every bid restarts it.

Playing XI and scoring
  • Your best 11 players are picked for you, with at most 4 overseas.
  • A good XI has 1 keeper, 3 specialist bowlers and 2 specialist batters. Each missing role costs 6 points.
  • Team total is the sum of the 11 ratings. The average gives your grade, S to D.
  • Ratings out of 99 are a fan-made estimate from each player's category. They are hidden until the auction ends.
Running a room
  • The host creates a room and shares the code. Friends open the game, tap Join and enter it.
  • Each person picks a different franchise. The host can spectate instead of bidding.
  • Between players the room waits for the SOLD or UNSOLD animation to finish.
  • Friends must be signed in to Claude and have Contributor access to bid. People with view-only access can watch.
