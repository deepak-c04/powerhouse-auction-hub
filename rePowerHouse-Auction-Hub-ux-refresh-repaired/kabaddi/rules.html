AUCTION RULES

Money
  • Purse: ₹100 Cr per franchise (₹80 Cr or ₹100 Cr in setup)
  • Base prices: Category A ₹2 Cr, Category B ₹1 Cr, Category C ₹25 L
  • Bid steps: ₹5 L up to ₹50 L, ₹10 L up to ₹2 Cr, ₹25 L up to ₹5 Cr, ₹50 L up to ₹10 Cr, then ₹1 Cr
  • Reserve: you must keep ₹25 L for every empty squad slot
Squad
  • Squad size: 8, 12, 18 or 25 players. 18–25 is the real PKL range
  • International: at least 2 per squad, otherwise −6
  • Starting 7: 2 raiders, 2 defenders, 1 all-rounder minimum, −6 for each missing role
  • Pool: all 235 players in the real Season 12 squads plus 8 retired legends
