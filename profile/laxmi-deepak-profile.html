The Story

Main character energy, student budget.

Between lectures at BITS I build things for fun: auction rooms where you and your friends fight over IPL, WPL, Kabaddi and BBL players with a fake purse and very real trash talk. No investors, no sleep. Just curiosity, a stubborn streak and my co-founder, Varshith.

  • 01

    Build it before it makes sense

    If an idea won't leave my head, it becomes a prototype by the weekend.

  • 02

    Friends are the focus group

    If the group chat bids for ten minutes straight, I know it works.

  • 03

    Ship messy, glow up later

    Version one is allowed to be chaos. Version ten gets the polish.

  • 04

    Stay playful, stay serious

    It's a game. I still sweat every pixel.
